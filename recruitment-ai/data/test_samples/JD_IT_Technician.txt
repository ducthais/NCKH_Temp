POSITION: IT SUPPORT ENGINEER / IT TECHNICIAN

JOB DESCRIPTION:
- Provide technical support for hardware and software issues across the organization
- Troubleshoot network connectivity problems, hardware malfunctions, and software errors
- Install, configure and maintain computer systems, servers and network equipment
- Manage IT help desk tickets and ensure timely resolution of user issues
- Collaborate with development teams to deploy and maintain web applications
- Document IT procedures and maintain knowledge base
- Support digital transformation initiatives and cloud migration projects

REQUIREMENTS:
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or related field
- At least 1 year of experience in IT support or system administration
- Proficiency in troubleshooting hardware and software problems
- Knowledge of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPN)
- Experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript for basic web support
- Familiarity with Python or other scripting languages for automation
- Strong communication and customer service skills
- English proficiency for technical documentation

PREFERRED:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Knowledge of cybersecurity best practices
- Cisco certifications (CCNA, CCT) or equivalent
- Experience with Linux server administration
- Understanding of digital marketing tools and analytics
- Project management experience
